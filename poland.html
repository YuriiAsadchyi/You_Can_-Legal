Poland Work Opportunity

Warehouse Worker (Magazynier)

Position & Industry

  • Position: Warehouse Worker (Magazynier)
  • Industry: Logistics / Warehousing — clothing, shoes, household goods and e-commerce
  • Location: Poland; exact city is assigned after arrival and onboarding

Job Responsibilities

  • Order picking with a scanner
  • Packing and preparing shipments
  • Receiving and sorting goods
  • Quality control
  • Removing security tags when required
  • Training is provided; Polish language and previous experience are not required

Working Schedule

  • 4–6 days per week
  • 8–12 hours per shift
  • Average 200–280 hours per month, depending on workload

Salary & Bonuses

  • 24.50–25.00 PLN net/hour
  • Students under 26: up to 30.50 PLN net/hour
  • Average monthly income: 4,500–7,500+ PLN net
  • Bonuses: up to 1,000 PLN
  • Salary is paid monthly; an advance is available after the first working week

Accommodation

  • Employer-provided housing
  • 3–4 people per room
  • Approximately 550 PLN/month, deducted from salary
  • Equipped apartments
  • Private housing may be partially or fully compensated depending on company policy

Contract & Support

  • Contract: Umowa Zlecenia
  • Assistance with Work Permit Type A
  • Assistance with Karta Pobytu
  • NFZ medical insurance support

Requirements

  • Age: 18–55
  • Good physical condition
  • Willingness to work and learn
  • No criminal record

Employment Term

  • Minimum 1 year
  • Long-term employment available
  • Work can start shortly after arrival and onboarding
Important information: This page presents the program information supplied for this opportunity. No sponsorship is provided and the programs are paid. Immigration, visa and residence decisions are made by the relevant authorities and requirements can change.
